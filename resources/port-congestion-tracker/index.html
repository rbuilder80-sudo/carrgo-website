Methodology and limitations

Public-source evidence review, not an AIS feed. Notices support only their stated location and period. A notice is not a measurement of port-wide congestion. Missing measurements remain null; no score, ranking, forecast or historical change is calculated. Health scores run high-is-good; congestion scores run high-is-bad. Neither is currently verified.
