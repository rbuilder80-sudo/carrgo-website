How the health score is calculated

The Carrgo port health score is a 0-100 composite of three measured inputs: the number of vessels waiting for a berth, berth utilisation as a percentage, and the average waiting time before a vessel is worked. A score above 75 means normal operations, 50-74 means moderate delay risk, and below 50 means the port is congested and importers should expect knock-on delays to inland delivery.

What importers should do about it

If your cargo is routed through a congested port

Check the alternative. A port scoring 40 points higher on the same coast frequently clears cargo days faster, and the re-routing cost is often lower than the demurrage you would pay waiting. Use the port comparison tool to compare two ports side by side, then price the difference with the freight cost calculator.

If you are planning a shipment

Book against current conditions rather than last quarter's assumptions. Congestion at UK ports moves week to week, which is why this report is republished weekly rather than left static.
