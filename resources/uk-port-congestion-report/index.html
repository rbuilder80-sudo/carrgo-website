Weekly evidence summary

Liverpool’s official notice page still lists local dock, bridge and lock notices. The Canada 90-foot Swing Bridge and Alfred Lock notices had time-limited windows that ended before this report was published; the source does not confirm actual reopening, so operators should verify locally. Royal Seaforth S7/S8 and the South Gladstone south quay remain within their stated closure periods. Dublin Port continues to list maintenance dredging and survey work. Met Office marine forecasts issued on 18 September carry Irish Sea and nearby coastal gale or strong-wind conditions. These are operational constraints and weather context—not measurements of port-wide congestion.

What changed over the past seven days

  • New Liverpool restrictions were recorded. Notice 62 covered Canada 90-foot Swing Bridge on 17 September; Notice 63 covered Alfred Lock on the 18 September high-water tide. Both stated windows had ended by publication, but no independent reopening confirmation was located.
  • Several earlier Liverpool windows ended. The Langton Lock schedule in Notice 43 ended on 11 September, Notice 60’s A-Bridge window ended on 14 September and Notice 59’s Gladstone Lock window ended on 15 September.
  • Ongoing Liverpool berth/dock restrictions remain. Notices 55 and 57 continue within their stated periods.
  • Dublin’s public notice position is materially unchanged. Dredging and survey notices remain listed; the conflict between two public Notice 39.3 end dates is unresolved.
  • Marine weather deteriorated for this report’s publication window. The current Met Office forecast includes Irish Sea gale conditions and strong winds around nearby coasts. This is weather context only; no carrier cancellation or port-wide delay was inferred.

No numerical week-on-week congestion change is calculated because there are no comparable verified port-wide measurements for either week.

Questions to ask before booking or collection

  1. Has the vessel schedule or terminal cut-off changed since the booking confirmation?
  2. Does a lock, bridge, dredging or survey notice affect this vessel, berth or collection route?
  3. Is a vehicle appointment required, and is the slot still confirmed?
  4. Are customs release, port health or transit documents complete before dispatch?
  5. When does free time begin, and who bears demurrage, detention or storage if the plan changes?

Methodology and limitations

Carrgo reviews dated public primary sources and records each supported claim at the narrowest defensible level. Port or terminal notices support only the location and period they name. Weather forecasts describe sea or coastal areas and are not treated as evidence of terminal queues. Carrgo does not currently use a verified AIS feed and does not calculate a numerical congestion, health, queue or berth-utilisation score without traceable measurements and a reproducible method. Missing values remain unknown. Advice is separated from facts, and users should confirm time-sensitive arrangements with the relevant carrier, terminal, port or VTS.
