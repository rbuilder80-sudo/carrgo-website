Correction: 2 September port snapshot

Withdrawn on 21 September 2026.

This page previously described hard-coded health scores, waiting times, vessel queues and berth-utilisation figures as current port measurements. Carrgo has no timestamped primary evidence or documented reproducible method supporting those numerical claims.

The unsupported figures have been removed. This URL remains available as a correction record and is excluded from search indexing.

Operational notices and weather advisories describe only their stated place and time. They do not establish port-wide congestion.
