Container Shipping Companies: Why Most Businesses Don't Book With Them Directly

Container shipping lines operate vessels. Freight forwarders arrange the wider journey around those vessels. For many UK SMEs, that difference determines whether the quote covers only port-to-port transport or the complete shipment.

Why going direct is not always the best fit

Major carriers are designed to sell vessel capacity. Some now offer digital bookings to smaller shippers, but an importer still needs to understand origin handling, export formalities, UK customs, port charges and final delivery.

For small volumes, the bigger issue is consolidation: if you only have a few pallets, you normally need LCL container shipping rather than a whole box.

What a freight forwarder adds

A forwarder books carrier space on the shipper's behalf and coordinates the services around it: documentation, consolidation, customs clearance, delivery and exception management. Carrgo does not own container vessels; we arrange the transport through carriers appropriate to the route and cargo.

FCL and LCL explained

FCL (Full Container Load) gives one shipper the whole container. LCL (Less than Container Load) combines several shippers' cargo in one container and charges each customer for the space used. The right choice depends on volume, density, handling sensitivity, timing and current market rates.

For most lanes, LCL is attractive for smaller consignments, while FCL becomes more economical as volume approaches a significant share of a container.

Example: importing furniture from China

A furniture importer may use FCL when the order fills most of a container. Carrgo can arrange the origin movement, carrier booking, sea freight, UK customs release and delivery to the warehouse. If the order is much smaller, the same lane can be handled as LCL so the importer does not pay for unused container space.

When direct carrier contracting can make sense

Large shippers moving consistent weekly container volumes may negotiate directly with shipping lines. Even then, many still use forwarders or brokers for customs, origin coordination and inland transport.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a small business book directly with a major carrier?

Some carriers offer direct online booking, but the importer still needs to manage the surrounding customs, documentation and inland transport scope.

What is the difference between FCL and LCL?

FCL means exclusive use of a container. LCL means your cargo shares a container with other shippers and you pay for the space used.

Does a freight forwarder always cost more than booking direct?

Not necessarily. The useful comparison is total landed transport cost, including origin charges, destination charges, customs and inland delivery.

How do I know whether I need FCL or LCL?

It depends on volume, weight, commodity, handling requirements and current rates. A forwarder can compare both options for the same shipment.
