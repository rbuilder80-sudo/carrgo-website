Air Freight Services: When Speed Actually Justifies the Cost

Air freight costs more than sea freight, but it can be the rational choice when a delay costs more than the transport. The decision should be based on the commercial cost of being late, not freight price alone.

What's included in door-to-door air freight

A properly scoped door-to-door air freight service can include supplier collection, origin handling, air waybill documentation, airline transport, UK customs clearance and final delivery. The air waybill identifies the shipment and contract of carriage and must be consistent with the commercial documents.

Express or economy: which do you actually need?

Express air freight is for genuinely time-critical cargo such as a spare part stopping production, samples required for a deadline or stock that has already run out. Economy air freight can still move much faster than sea, but with a lower priority and often a better rate.

The right choice depends on the latest acceptable delivery date, not simply whether the shipment feels urgent.

The part that catches importers out: chargeable weight

Air freight is commonly rated on chargeable weight, which is the greater of actual gross weight and volumetric weight under the applicable airline or consolidator formula. Bulky, lightweight goods can therefore cost more than expected because aircraft capacity is limited by both mass and space.

Provide accurate carton dimensions and gross weights when requesting a quote. Our quote form can be used even if some dimensions are still approximate.

Who uses air freight regularly

Typical users include ecommerce and Amazon FBA sellers protecting stock availability, manufacturers waiting on components, wholesalers covering a stock gap and retailers moving time-sensitive seasonal inventory. In each case, the premium makes sense only if faster arrival protects revenue, production or customer commitments.

Air freight versus sea freight

Sea freight is normally the lower-cost choice for non-urgent volume. Air freight is best when speed and predictability outweigh unit freight cost. For urgent and non-urgent portions of the same purchase order, splitting the shipment can sometimes be more economical than flying everything.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does door-to-door air freight take?

Transit depends on origin, destination, flight availability, customs and final delivery. Major international lanes are usually measured in days rather than weeks.

What is the difference between express and economy air freight?

Express prioritises speed and connection availability. Economy air freight accepts more flexibility in exchange for a lower rate.

What is volumetric weight?

It converts a shipment's dimensions into a weight equivalent. Air freight is generally charged on the higher of actual weight and volumetric weight under the carrier's formula.

Can Carrgo handle customs clearance for air freight?

Yes. UK customs clearance can be managed as part of the same air freight movement.

Is air freight only for large companies?

No. A small urgent shipment can justify air freight if the commercial cost of delay is high enough.
